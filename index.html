A SCHOOL WITH A PURPOSE

Education should open doors for every child.

Rudra Goswami Public School (RGPS) is located in Vahelal, Ahmedabad. The school provides education from Nursery to Standard 10 under the GSEB board, with a mission focused on supporting rural education.

OUR COMMITMENT

Education for everyone,
opportunity for each.

Additional funding supports school operations and helps sustain learning opportunities. We believe a child’s future should not be limited by where they are born or their family’s financial circumstances.
