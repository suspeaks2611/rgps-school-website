OUR MISSION

Every Child Deserves the Opportunity to Learn.

Education should not be limited by where a child is born or the financial circumstances of their family.

RGPS school photograph · not an RGPS student
WHY IT MATTERS

Place should never define a child’s potential.

Rural communities can face barriers to accessing quality education. Families may have fewer nearby options, and the cost of schooling can make learning harder to sustain. These challenges are broad and varied; each community’s experience is different.

Accessible education gives children a foundation to learn, explore their abilities and imagine new possibilities for the future.
